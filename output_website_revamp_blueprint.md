## 1. BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN SYSTEM

*Preserving official Brand Logo and Tagline while elevating the visual hierarchy to world-class DTC standards.*

### Color Palette (Rule #1)
- **Primary Terracotta (`#C86432`)**: Used for primary call-to-action buttons, high-urgency badges, key metric highlights, and active borders.
- **Espresso Text (`#37281D`)**: Deep, rich contrast for headlines, navigation items, and primary typography.
- **Warm Cream / Ivory Background (`#FAF8F5`)**: Soft, clean backdrop that creates an organic, veterinary-grade feeling without the harshness of pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Accent Muted Gold (`#E29D38`)**: Star ratings, verified badges, and discount ribbons.
- **Accent Sage Green (`#4A7C59` / `#EBF2ED`)**: Fast US shipping pills, organic trust seals, and veterinarian approval stamps.

### Typography
- **Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 / 800 weight, `-0.5px` to `-1px` letter-spacing.
- **Body Text**: `Inter`, 400 / 500 / 600 weight, `1.6` line-height.

## 5. SHOPIFY THEME 2.0 (OS 2.0) LIQUID IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE

The revamped storefront sections are modularized and ready for drop-in use into your Shopify theme:

1. **`storefront/shopify_theme_sections/hero-cloud-bed.liquid`**: Drop into `sections/hero-cloud-bed.liquid`.
2. **`storefront/shopify_theme_sections/bundle-tiers.liquid`**: Drop into `sections/bundle-tiers.liquid`.

To add them in Shopify:
1. In Shopify Admin, navigate to **Online Store** $\rightarrow$ **Themes** $\rightarrow$ **Edit code**.
2. Under **Sections**, click *Add a new section*, name it `hero-cloud-bed.liquid`, and paste the code.
3. Open the **Theme Customizer**, click **Add section**, and select **PawPawDoo Hero Banner** and **PawPawDoo Bundle Tiers**!
