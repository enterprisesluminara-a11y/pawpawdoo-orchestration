Frequently Asked Questions & Sizing

The easiest way is to measure your pet while they are curled up asleep from nose to tail:

  • 40cm (16"): Best for kittens and cats up to 10 lbs.
  • 50cm (20"): Best for larger cats, Shih Tzus, Chihuahuas (up to 18 lbs).
  • 60cm (24"): Best for Frenchies, Pugs, Corgis, and multi-cat cuddles (18–25 lbs).
  • 80cm (32"): Best for Beagles, Spaniels, Aussies, and medium dogs (25–45 lbs).

Tip: If your pet loves to stretch out or is between sizes, we recommend sizing up!
